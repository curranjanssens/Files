# Novel Tryptamine for Cluster Headache: Development Program

## Disease Context

**Cluster Headache**
- Prevalence: ~0.1% (rare disease threshold: <200,000 US patients)
- Severity: "Suicide headache" - among most painful conditions known
- Unmet need: 10-20% refractory to all approved treatments
- Patient population: Desperate, organized, willing to self-experiment
- Endogenous tryptamine may play role in pathogenesis and bout duration

**Current Evidence for Tryptamines (2024-2025 Updates)**

| Compound | Trial Status | Key Results |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Psilocybin | Phase 2 RCT completed (NCT02981173) | Moderate effect size (d=0.69), large in chronic (d=1.25); effects NOT correlated with psychedelic intensity |
| Psilocybin | PEACE Trial (Australia) | 10mg weekly x4 vs placebo, newly funded |
| LSD | Phase 2 (NCT03781128) | Active |
| BOL-148 | Phase 1 completed | 3 doses over 10 days reduced frequency/intensity |

**Critical Finding**: Psilocybin's headache benefits are INDEPENDENT of acute psychedelic effects → non-hallucinogenic analogs may retain efficacy

Sources:
- [PubMed - Psilocybin Pulse Regimen](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36416492/)
- [George Institute - PEACE Trial](https://www.georgeinstitute.org/news-and-media/news/hope-for-cluster-headache-community-as-psilocybin-trial-funded)

## Mechanism Considerations

### Two Distinct Mechanisms at Play

| Mechanism | Receptor | Effect | Examples |
|-----------|----------|--------|----------|
| Acute abortion | 5-HT1B/1D | Vasoconstriction, trigeminal inhibition | Sumatriptan, zolmitriptan |
| Prophylactic/disease-modifying | 5-HT2A | Neuroplasticity, cortical effects | Psilocybin, LSD, DMT |

**Key insight**: Psychedelic tryptamines appear to PREVENT cycles, not just abort attacks. This is different from triptans.

### Target Product Profile Options

**Option A: Non-hallucinogenic 5-HT2A agonist (prophylactic)**
- Retain cluster-breaking efficacy
- Remove psychoactive effects
- Precedent: BOL-148, tabernanthalog

**Option B: Novel 5-HT1B/1D/1F agonist (acute)**
- Compete with triptans
- Harder to differentiate
- Crowded IP space

**Option C: Dual 5-HT2A/1B agonist**
- Both acute and prophylactic
- Novel mechanism
- Higher complexity

**Recommendation**: Option A - non-hallucinogenic 5-HT2A agonist for prophylaxis

## Compound Design Principles

### Avoiding Scheduled Status

The Federal Analogue Act covers substances "substantially similar" to Schedule I/II drugs. Key considerations:

**Structural features to AVOID**:
- Direct 4-substituted tryptamines (psilocybin analogs)
- N,N-dialkyl tryptamines (DMT analogs)
- Lysergamide core (LSD analogs)

**Potentially SAFER structural space**:
- 5-substituted tryptamines (serotonin-like)
- 6- or 7-substituted tryptamines
- β-carboline derivatives
- Conformationally constrained analogs
- Prodrug strategies

### Patentability Considerations

**Freedom to operate concerns**:
- Usona Institute: Psilocybin formulations
- Compass Pathways: Crystalline psilocybin
- Various: 4-substituted tryptamine derivatives

**Novel chemical space**:
- Unusual substitution patterns (6-position, 7-position)
- Heterocyclic modifications
- Biased agonism (G-protein vs β-arrestin)
- Deuterated analogs (extend half-life, novel composition)

## Proposed Candidate Structures (10 Compounds)

### Design Informed by Recent Patents & Non-Hallucinogenic Literature

**Key insight from WO 2025/024400**: 2,7-dialkyl substitution produces potent 5-HT2A agonism with reduced 5-HT2B activity (cardiotoxicity concern) and potentially reduced psychoactivity

**Key insight from BOL-148**: Biased partial agonism (G-protein vs β-arrestin) may separate therapeutic from hallucinogenic effects. BOL-148 EC50 = 0.81 nM, Emax = 60% (vs LSD Emax = 92%)

**Key insight from Tabernanthalog**: Water solubility, 5-HT2A agonism + 5-HT2B antagonism = good safety profile

### Tier 1: Lead Candidates (4)

**1. 2,7-Dimethyl-4-hydroxy-DET analog**
- Rationale: Directly informed by WO 2025/024400 patent
- Expected: Submicromolar 5-HT2A EC50, reduced 5-HT2B, neuroplastogenic
- IP status: May need to design around existing claims
- Scheduling risk: LOW (novel structure, no direct scheduled analog)

**2. 6-Fluoro-4-hydroxy-tryptamine (6F-psilocin scaffold)**
- Rationale: 6-position F blocks metabolic hotspot, extends half-life
- Expected: Retained 5-HT2A, potentially reduced BBB penetration
- IP status: Novel
- Scheduling risk: MEDIUM (4-hydroxy = psilocin pharmacophore)

**3. 7-Methyl-N,N-diethyltryptamine (7-Me-DET)**
- Rationale: 7-position rarely explored, DET less potent than DMT
- Expected: Modified receptor kinetics, biased agonism
- IP status: Novel
- Scheduling risk: LOW

**4. 2-Bromo-4-hydroxy-N-methyltryptamine (BOL-psilocin hybrid)**
- Rationale: Combine BOL-148's biased agonism with psilocin's CH efficacy
- Expected: Partial agonist, non-hallucinogenic, neuroplastogenic
- IP status: Novel
- Scheduling risk: MEDIUM

### Tier 2: Backup Candidates (4)

**5. 4-Acetoxy-7-methyl-tryptamine**
- Prodrug strategy (like psilocybin → psilocin)
- Water soluble, oral bioavailable

**6. N-Cyclopropyl-5-MeO-tryptamine**
- Constrained N-alkyl, different receptor engagement
- 5-MeO position (not 4-position = different pharmacophore)

**7. Deuterated 4-HO-MET (d6)**
- Extended half-life via deuterium kinetic isotope effect
- Novel composition of matter patent

**8. Tryptamine-2-carboxylic acid ethyl ester**
- Reduced BBB penetration
- Peripheral selectivity if needed

### Tier 3: Exploratory (2)

**9. 6-Methoxy-2-methyl-tryptamine**
- Minimal structural similarity to known scheduled compounds
- Novel pharmacology to explore

**10. β-Carboline-tryptamine hybrid (harmine analog)**
- Rigidified, different binding mode
- MAO-A inhibitor + 5-HT2A agonist dual mechanism

## 2. DEA & Scheduling Considerations

### Federal Analogue Act - Critical Details

The Act covers substances "substantially similar" to Schedule I/II drugs, **but only if intended for human consumption**.

**Key precedent** (*United States v. Forbes*, 1992):
- Court ruled alphaethyltryptamine (AET) was NOT substantially similar to DMT/DET
- Reasoning: Different amine type (primary vs tertiary), dissimilar hallucinogenic effects
- **Implication**: Structural novelty + different pharmacological profile = stronger defense

**Research Protection**:
> "This legislation is not aimed at legitimate drug research that unwittingly falls within the designer drug definition" - Sen. Joe Biden (1986)

Early-stage clinical research is explicitly protected.

### Recent DEA Developments (2022-2024)

DEA **withdrew** proposed scheduling of 5 psychedelic tryptamines after challenges:
- 4-OH-DiPT
- 5-MeO-AMT
- 5-MeO-MiPT
- 5-MeO-DET
- DiPT

**Status**: DEA requested new HHS scientific evaluation - these remain **unscheduled** as of now.

Source: [Federal Register - DEA Withdrawal](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2022/07/27/2022-16102/schedules-of-controlled-substances-placement-of-4-hydroxy-nn-diisopropyltryptamine-4-oh-dipt)

## 4. Toxicology Considerations

### 5-HT2B Receptor - The Critical Safety Target

**Background**: 5-HT2B agonism causes cardiac valvulopathy (fenfluramine, pergolide disasters).

**Mechanism**: Chronic 5-HT2B activation → myofibroblast proliferation → valve fibrosis → regurgitation

**Timeline for toxicity**: 4-6 months of chronic high-dose exposure (pergolide data)

**Psychedelic dosing advantage**: Intermittent "pulse" dosing (3 doses, 5 days apart) ≠ chronic exposure

### Compound Screening Strategy

| Assay | Target | Acceptable Ratio |
|-------|--------|------------------|
| 5-HT2A functional EC50 | Therapeutic target | - |
| 5-HT2B binding Ki | Safety anti-target | >100-fold vs 5-HT2A |
| hERG IC50 | Cardiac arrhythmia | >30-fold vs therapeutic Cmax |

**FDA recommendation**: Use **binding assays (Ki)** not functional assays for 5-HT2B safety margin.

**Design insight**: 2,7-dialkyl tryptamines show reduced 5-HT2B activity (WO 2025/024400).

Source: [Expert Opinion - 5-HT2B and Psilocybin](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14740338.2023.2248883)

## 5. Head-Twitch Response (HTR) - Key Preclinical Assay

### Predictive Value

The mouse HTR is the **gold standard** for predicting human hallucinogenic potential:
- **High construct validity**: 5-HT2A receptor-mediated
- **High predictive validity**: Potency correlates with human subjective effects
- All known hallucinogens induce HTR; non-hallucinogens (lisuride, ergotamine) do NOT

## 6. Clinical Trial Design

### Recommended Endpoints (IHS Guidelines 2024)

**Primary**: Change from baseline in weekly attack frequency

**Challenges**:
- Natural remission in episodic CH confounds placebo comparison
- After week 4, treatment and placebo groups tend to converge

**Solutions**:
- Enroll at **onset of cluster period** (minimizes placebo effect)
- Use **short treatment periods** (4 weeks maximum for ECH)
- **50% responder rate** as key secondary endpoint
- Consider **30%, 75%, 100% response rates** for secondary analysis

**Sample sizes**: N=10-20 may show signal in rare disease context

### Psilocybin Protocol Precedent

Yale/Schindler protocol:
- 3 doses, 5 days apart ("pulse" regimen)
- Low dose (10mg/70kg for psilocybin equivalent)
- 8-week follow-up
- Effect size d=0.69 (moderate); d=1.25 in chronic patients

Source: [PMC - Commentary on CH Trial Guidelines](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10921584/)

## 8. Synthesis & Formulation

### Synthesis Routes

**Speeter-Anthony route** (standard tryptamine synthesis):
- Start from indole
- Well-established, scalable
- Usona published open-source psilocybin synthesis

**Cost factors**:
- Number of steps
- Starting material costs
- Purity requirements
- Scale (mg vs g vs kg)
- Novel vs known compounds

**Estimate**: $3-5k per novel compound at 10-50g scale with in-house chemist

### Prodrug Strategy

**Why prodrugs?**
- 4-hydroxy tryptamines are unstable (oxidation)
- Phosphate esters (psilocybin) or acetate esters (psilacetin) improve stability
- Psilocybin bioavailability: ~53%
- Complete conversion to psilocin in first pass

**Psilacetin (4-AcO-DMT) advantages**:
- Simpler synthesis than psilocybin (no phosphate group)
- Superior atom economy
- Disclosed in 1961 Hofmann patent

Source: [ACS - Psychedelic Prodrug Advances](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsmedchemlett.4c00519)
