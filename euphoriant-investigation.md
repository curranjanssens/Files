# In Vivo Evidence for a Sustainable, Reliable Euphoriant

*An educational neuroscience/pharmacology literature synthesis. Mechanism and
published evidence only — no acquisition, dosing, or synthesis guidance.
Compiled June 2026.*

---

## Question

Does in vivo evidence (human and animal) support the existence of any
sustainable, reliable euphoriant — in any format (pharmacological, behavioral,
neuromodulatory) — that produces euphoria reliably and keeps doing so across
repeated use without tolerance?

## The unified finding

Across every branch investigated, the evidence converges on one principle:

> **Tolerance is recruited by the acute euphoric "a-process" and its repetition,
> via the dynorphin/CRF antireward system and reward-circuit downregulation.
> Therefore every durable, non-tolerating intervention in the literature works
> by *not* producing a strong euphoric spike** — it restores a depressed baseline
> toward normal, stays in a sub-saturating partial-tone window, remodels the
> circuit once and infrequently, or generates felt intensity without reward
> amplitude. The one thing none of them sustains is a repeated, supranormal
> euphoric high — because that is the precise stimulus that recruits its own
> cancellation.

The deciding variable is **not** ligand identity, endogenous-vs-exogenous,
phasic-vs-tonic, or even neuroplasticity. It is whether the agent produces acute
euphoric reward, and how often it is dosed.

## The four mechanisms that oppose sustained euphoria

1. **Opponent-process theory** (Solomon & Corbit 1974, [PMID 4817611](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4817611/)):
   every pleasure (a-process) recruits a slower opposite reaction (b-process)
   that strengthens with repetition. Net euphoria shrinks; withdrawal grows.
2. **Allostasis / set-point shift** (Koob & Le Moal 2001, [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/1395603)):
   repeated reward both blunts the reward system ("within-system") and recruits
   an active anti-reward stress system (CRF, dynorphin/KOR; "between-system"),
   shifting the hedonic baseline *downward*. ICSS reward thresholds rise
   cumulatively and fail to reset between sessions.
3. **Reward prediction error** (Schultz, [PMC4760620](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4760620/)):
   dopamine signals *surprise*, not reward. A fully predicted reward produces
   almost no signal — euphoria is parasitic on novelty.
4. **Receptor downregulation**: 5-HT2A (psychedelics), CB1 (cannabis/2-AG),
   D2 (stimulants), SERT/serotonin depletion (MDMA).

## Map of the search space

### Dead axis — reward amplitude (the euphoric flood)

- **Opioids, stimulants**: reliable acute euphoria, but euphoria is among the
  *first* effects lost to tolerance. Human acute tolerance to cocaine "high" at
  constant plasma levels ([PMID 3390996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3390996/));
  d-amphetamine subjective tolerance ([PMID 8834422](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8834422/)).
- **Endogenous-release drugs disprove the "endogenous = safe" hypothesis**:
  amphetamine (dopamine) and MDMA (serotonin) release the brain's *own*
  transmitters via transporter reversal, which empties the releasable pool and
  bypasses autoreceptor brakes. The decisive experiment: MDMA-tolerant rats are
  tolerant to other *releasers* but not to a direct receptor agonist (m-CPP) —
  tolerance tracks lost release capacity ([PMID 20477756](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20477756/)).
  Worse, faster rate-of-rise *increases* sensitization ([JNeurosci](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/24/28/6362)),
  so a sharp phasic spike is a tolerance *risk factor*, not protection.
- **MDMA "loses the magic"**: serotonin depletion + TPH inhibition; reduced SERT
  binding in regular users ([PMC2034411](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2034411/)).
- **DFNZ** (μ-opioid superagonist, *Nature* April 2026): tolerance-free analgesia
  in rodents *precisely because* it produces flat/slow, not phasic, dopamine —
  i.e., it works by lacking euphoria ([Medscape](https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/novel-opioid-offers-potent-pain-relief-without-classic-side-2026a1000age)).

### Brain stimulation / DBS — "wanting" without "liking"

- Direct reward stimulation drives compulsive *seeking*, not satisfaction
  (Olds-Milner; Heath B-19; Portenoy 1986 thalamic patient, [PMID 3492699](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3492699/)).
  Human NAc electrodes "explicitly failed to produce pleasure."
- Berridge & Kringelbach 2015 ([PMC4425246](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4425246/)):
  dopamine mediates "wanting," not "liking." Confirmed bidirectionally —
  ~99% dopamine depletion leaves hedonic "liking" intact; hyperdopaminergic mice
  show more "wanting" but not "liking" ([J Neurosci](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/23/28/9395)).

### Target A — lower the noise floor (signal-to-noise)

- Hagerty et al. 2013 ([PMC3659471](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3659471/)):
  a jhana adept self-activates the nucleus accumbens on demand; extreme bliss
  with only *modest* NAc activation, attributed to a lowered cortical noise floor
  rather than amplified reward. Practitioners report "negative tolerance."
- **The only route in the entire map with a negative-tolerance signature.**
- BUT: n=1, an internal within-session NAc-depletion caveat in the 5th jhana,
  no direct tolerance test exists, and natural rewards *do* habituate
  ([PMID 34846754](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34846754/)). **No drug is shown
  to produce pleasure primarily by noise reduction.** Most theoretically
  attractive, most under-evidenced. Genuinely open.
- Float/sensory-deprivation supports the "quiet the noise → modest signal reads
  loud" half (Feinstein 2018, [PMC6040829](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6040829/)),
  but the rigorous placebo-controlled RCT (Borg 2021, [PMC8122226](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8122226/))
  shows acute effects largely matched by placebo.

### Target B — raise / unblock the baseline (partial-tone window)

- **Endocannabinoid / FAAH (Jo Cameron)**: lifelong elevated anandamide →
  durable low-anxiety, positive-disposition phenotype with no tolerance
  (Habib 2019, [PMC6676009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6676009/);
  mechanism Mikaeili 2023, [PMC10473560](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10473560/)).
  But this is lifelong genomic remodeling (FAAH-OUT lncRNA + ~797 genes), not
  "more anandamide" — and **every FAAH-inhibitor mood/anxiety trial failed**
  (PF-04457845, JNJ-42165279, JZP150). BIA 10-2474's deaths were a
  compound-specific off-target effect, not a class verdict ([Science 2017](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aaf7497)).
- **The dose-response law** (Schlosburg 2010, [PMC2928870](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2928870/)):
  raising 2-AG (CB1 *full* agonist) via complete MAGL inhibition →
  downregulation, tolerance, dependence; raising anandamide (*partial* agonist)
  or low-dose/partial MAGL inhibition → durable benefit, CB1 intact. Endocannabinoid
  elevation is a tolerance-free lever *only inside a bounded partial-tone window*.
- **KOR antagonists** (aticaprant, navacaprant): block the dynorphin anti-reward
  brake. FAST-MAS proof-of-mechanism *restored* ventral-striatal reward signaling
  with "unaltered reward sensitivity" — higher learning rate, not amplified
  hedonics ([PMC7419512](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7419512/)). NAc
  antagonist raises dopamine to ~175% of baseline vs 500–1000% for cocaine. No
  euphoria, no abuse signal. **But both failed Phase 3** (navacaprant KOASTAL-1
  Jan 2025; aticaprant VENTURA Mar 2025), large placebo responses.
- **Tianeptine**: the law in one molecule. Same μ-opioid agonism; at low
  therapeutic dose restores baseline with no tolerance, at euphoric/abuse dose
  produces morphine-like CPP, tolerance, dependence ("gas station heroin")
  ([PMC4119213](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4119213/); [CDC MMWR](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/67/wr/mm6730a2.htm)).
- **Pramipexole** (D3-preferring agonist): *normalized* blunted striatal reward
  processing in anhedonia (PRIME-PRAXOL, SHAPS g=0.63), no mania, restoration not
  overshoot ([PMC9534553](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9534553/)).
- **Lithium, agomelatine, conventional ADs**: durable mood support precisely
  because there is no euphoric a-process; SSRIs in fact *blunt* reward bidirectionally
  ([PMC2828549](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2828549/)).

### Target C — amplify "liking" directly via hedonic hotspots

- Tiny (~1 mm³) opioid, endocannabinoid, and orexin hotspots in NAc shell /
  ventral pallidum / OFC / insula *double-to-triple* hedonic "liking" reactions,
  via a substrate **separate from** dopaminergic "wanting" (Castro & Berridge 2014,
  [J Neurosci](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/34/12/4239); Berridge & Kringelbach
  2015). Dopamine does not move "liking" at all.
- Distinct substrate → the amplitude/tolerance logic may not apply identically.
  **Tolerance-resistance untested; selective targeting with a systemic drug is the
  hard part** (hotspots act as a unanimous-vote network). Freshest unexplored cell.

### Target D — psychoplastogens (one-time remodeling)

- Ketamine, psychedelics, ibogaine produce lasting synaptogenesis (BDNF/TrkB/mTOR;
  intracellular 5-HT2A; VTA-GDNF) after one/few doses. Classic psychedelics fit best —
  weakly reinforcing, low abuse liability, *because* no strong dopaminergic a-process.
- **But not sufficient**: ketamine engages dopamine/opioid reward and shows abuse
  liability + tachyphylaxis; chronic DMT causes spine *retraction*. Dosed too often,
  even structural plasticity meets homeostatic correction. Durable effects are
  *remission toward euthymia* (psilocybin/ketamine for depression), not sustained euphoria.

## Existence proofs (the question is not "is it possible")

- **Jo Cameron**: sustained, lifelong, tolerance-free elevated wellbeing + painlessness
  (tonic endocannabinoid set-point).
- **Jhana**: reproducible, on-demand bliss with apparent negative tolerance (signal-to-noise).

Both demonstrate sustained reliable positive affect *is* biologically real — but
both are *baseline/relief or noise-floor* phenomena, and even jhana's masters
ultimately transition bliss into equanimity (the intense-pleasure component appears
to be the part most subject to adaptation, even endogenously).

## Honest bottom line

- **Durably raising the hedonic *baseline*** toward — and arguably modestly above —
  a depressed set-point, within a sub-euphoric partial-tone window, is real and
  partly reachable (Targets B/C/D). This is the genuine, humane goal for the ~1.5
  billion in chronic pain and for anhedonia/depression.
- **A sustained supranormal *euphoric high*** is not supported by the evidence,
  because the opponent process attaches to intense positive affect itself, not
  merely to the receptor mechanism producing it.
- **Genuinely open frontiers**: Target A (a molecule whose pleasure is a
  signal-to-noise effect) and Target C (hotspot-restricted "liking" without
  "wanting"). Neither has been tested. Not closed doors.
