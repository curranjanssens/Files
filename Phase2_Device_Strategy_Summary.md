### E-MESH: THE LEAN PATH

**What it is**: Rebuildable Dripping Atomizer (RDA) with SS316L mesh, temperature-controlled mod. DMT community gold standard.

**Efficiency**: ~95-100% vaporization, <5% residue (vs 30-50% for smoking, 70-85% for consumer vapes)

**Pre-loading protocol**:
```
PHARMACY: Analytical balance → 20.00mg ± 0.05mg → melt onto mesh → seal in RDA → package
PATIENT: Attach to locked mod → single 7-10s breath → hold 10s → exhale → return device
```

**Hardware** (all available 2025):
- Geekvape Aegis mod: $60-90
- Vandy Vape Mesh V2 RDA: $30-50
- SS316L mesh: $10/pack
- **Total setup: ~$100-150/patient**

**Settings**: TC mode, TCR 090-110, 190-210°C, 35-60W limit

### BOTTOM LINE

**E-Mesh is the lean path**: $30-50k to validate, 30-60s onset, ~100% vaporization efficiency, DMT community proof-of-concept. Risk: novel platform, needs characterization.

**Hovione DPI is the safe path**: $150-300k, proven regulatory acceptance, >50% lung deposition documented. Risk: slower onset (2-5 min), higher cost.

**Either works for Phase 2 exit**. Acquirer will likely re-evaluate device anyway. Your job: prove the molecule works. Device is replaceable.
