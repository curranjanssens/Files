# Real Mechanisms for "Potentiate-Then-Lose" Ultra-Low-Dose Windows

*Educational pharmacology / biophysics synthesis. Mechanism and published evidence only.
The phenomenon taken as REAL; asking the literature what genuinely produces it.
Independent of the retracted Crain/Wang/Burns filamin-A opioid lineage. June 2026.*

The phenomenon: an ultra-low antagonist (or partner) dose potentiates a co-applied agonist;
a higher dose loses the benefit (returns to baseline). The literature answers richly — with
a rigorous unifying principle and multiple independently-documented mechanisms.

## The unifying principle (a theorem, not hand-waving)

A single one-directional binding site **saturates monotonically** — it cannot make a curve
that rises and returns to baseline. "Potentiate-then-lose-then-flat" requires ONE of exactly
three architectures, all independently documented:

1. **Two opposite-sign sites of different affinity on one target.**
2. **Prozone / hook (bridging self-competition).**
3. **Dimer cross-protomer cooperativity** (partial/opposite partner-occupancy helps;
   full occupancy abolishes).

(A single allosteric site under the ternary-complex/operational model saturates; only
multi-site or bridging geometry yields an intrinsic bell — [PMC11389776](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11389776/).)

## Architecture 1 — two opposite-sign sites (cleanest worked examples)

- **Insulin-receptor aptamer IR-A62**: enhances binding at 3–80 nM, *suppresses below baseline*
  at 0.4–10 µM, by two-site self-competition on a dimeric receptor — exactly potentiate-then-lose
  ([PMC9076861](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9076861/)).
- **Zinc on glycine / NMDA receptors**: high-affinity potentiating site + low-affinity inhibitory
  site, both mutagenesis-mapped → net inverted-U ([GlyR PMC2269571](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2269571/),
  [NMDA PMC6333211](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6333211/)).
- **Ryanodine on RyR**: nM site activates (subconductance), µM site blocks, same protein
  ([PMC30231](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC30231/)).

## Architecture 2 — prozone / hook (bridging)

Rigorous math (Bray & Lay): a ligand bridging two partners gives maximal complex at intermediate
concentration, declining at high as half-occupied species dominate — floors at baseline, cannot
reverse ([PMC5556679](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5556679/)). The bivalent-ligand
family (MDAN-21, tolerance-free at ≥19-atom spacer) is the same geometry.

## Architecture 3 — dimer cross-protomer cooperativity (the GPCR demonstration)

- **D2 dopamine dimer (Han & Javitch 2009)**: agonist on one protomer = max signal; an
  **inverse agonist on the partner protomer POTENTIATES it to ~157%**; an agonist (or
  constitutive activity) on the partner **BLUNTS to ~50%**
  ([Nat Chem Biol](https://www.nature.com/articles/nchembio.199)). The clean GPCR analog of
  "low/opposite partner-occupancy helps, full occupancy abolishes" — in D2, a receptor in the pattern.
- **MOR–DOR heteromer (Devi/Gomes, independent of the fraud)**: sub-stoichiometric occupancy of
  ONE protomer by agonist OR antagonist allosterically potentiates the partner, only in
  co-expressing cells, by slowing partner ligand dissociation
  ([PMC4292954](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4292954/)).
- **Two-state dimer model** (Franco/Casadó/Ferré): the "dimer cooperativity index" and anomalous
  bell-shaped competition curves (A1, A2A, D1) are the documented signature
  ([PMC6360119](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6360119/)).
- Trans-inhibition at full occupancy is documented (5-HT7, [PMC3268885](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3268885/);
  α2A-MOR cross-inhibition). *Honest gap: the full single-biphasic-curve within one dimer driven
  by one titrated ligand is assembled from documented halves + models, never shown end-to-end.*

## The filamin-A reframe — substrate real, two joints fabricated

FLNA was dismissed by me on the fraud alone. The independent literature shows the substrate is
real and pervasive; only two specific joints are fabricated.

**Independently real (multiple labs):**
- **FLNA is a general GPCR scaffold** — binds D2/D3 (Ig19), CaSR, calcitonin, mGluR7, GABA-B,
  CXCR4, somatostatin, CFTR, and MOR (Onoprishvili/Simon, NYU — *independent*). A conserved
  filamin-binding motif occurs in **>20% of human GPCRs** (Karnik 2015, NMR/ITC,
  [PMC4642222](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4642222/)).
- **FLNA controls receptor internalization / recycling / desensitization** (receptor-specific
  direction): in FLNA-deficient cells, agonist-induced MOR desensitization/downregulation is
  *abolished* ([Onoprishvili, PMID 14573758](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14573758/)).
- **FLNA is a genuine two-state conformational switch**: closed (auto-inhibited, A-strand
  occludes the CD groove) vs open, ~10–17× affinity swing, gated by a **force window** (2–5 pN
  opens, >12 pN abolishes — itself biphasic), by **ligand binding relieving auto-inhibition**,
  and by a slow **cis/trans proline switch (Pro2225)** latching the state for minutes
  ([Lad 2007 PMC1948075](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1948075/);
  [Rognoni 2012 PMID 23150587](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23150587/);
  [Rognoni 2014 proline switch PMID 24706888](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24706888/)).

**The two fabricated / unsupported joints (exactly the load-bearing ones):**
1. **A small molecule shifting FLNA's conformational state** — the only candidate is simufilam,
   which IS the fraud. No independent demonstration of a small-molecule allosteric FLNA switch.
2. **FLNA's state translating into a receptor BINDING-AFFINITY change** — **no independent study
   shows FLNA altering D2 or MOR agonist affinity or high/low-affinity states.** All real
   FLNA-receptor work is trafficking/coupling, never affinity.

So: real, pervasive scaffold; real two-state switch; real desensitization control — but the
specific chain (drug→FLNA-state→receptor-affinity) is unproven and its data are fabricated.

## Bottom line

Oxytrex's "potentiate-then-lose" **does not need filamin A** — it has three independent,
rigorous mechanistic routes (two-opposite-sites; prozone; dimer cooperativity, demonstrated in
the D2 dimer). The phenomenon was never mechanistically absurd. A fraud planted a fake unifying
answer (FLNA two-site, pM/nM) on a real, pervasive scaffold and crowded out the legitimate,
plural explanation the wider literature supports. The genuinely missing work is mapping which
architecture operates in which system — a *grounded* prediction from established mechanism, not
a shot in the dark.
